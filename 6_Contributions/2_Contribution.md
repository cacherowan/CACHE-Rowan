    Dr. Kirti M. Yenkie is an Associate Professor of Chemical Engineering at Rowan University and leads the Sustainable Design and Systems Medicine lab. Her research and mentoring efforts are geared towards environmental sustainability by including practical examples and case studies from industry on water treatment, solvent recycling, petroleum pipeline cleaning, plastics recycling, and machine learning for early-stage life cycle analysis. She has developed educational modules, and videos for STEM outreach on Sustainability. She holds a Ph.D. in Bioengineering from the University of Illinois (Chicago, IL), M. Tech (Chem. Engg.) from IIT Bombay and B. Tech (Chem. Engg.) from Laxminarayan Institute of Technology, Nagpur. She has received the NSF CAREER award and multiple awards from the AIChE Divisions.

    Dr. Aditya Lele is an Assistant Professor of Mechanical Engineering at Rowan University, with a courtesy appointment in Chemical Engineering. His research uses ML-accelerated atomistic simulations to study dynamic, non-equilibrium, and reactive processes, with applications in plastic recycling, plasma catalysis, rapid joule-heated catalysis, and solid-state materials. He also teaches an interdisciplinary Practical Molecular Simulations course at Rowan.

    Dr. Robert P. Hesketh is an internationally recognized leader in chemical engineering education, research, and professional service. Since 2000, he has served as Professor of Chemical Engineering at Rowan University, where he was Department Chair (2004–2012). He has held faculty positions at the University of Tulsa and completed postdoctoral research at the University of Cambridge. Dr. Hesketh is widely known for pioneering the integration of hands-on, experiential learning into chemical engineering curricula, including innovative laboratory modules and the acclaimed Rowan Engineering Clinic model. He has secured over $5.5 million in external research funding, led interdisciplinary research projects in green engineering, separations, process engineering, and reaction engineering, and significantly advanced the application of sustainability principles in chemical engineering practice. 
      Dr. Hesketh has been a member of the AIChE North American Mixing Forum Board and served as Program Chair (2016–2022). As a founding member of the AIChE Chem-E-Car Competition committee, he has championed student engagement for over 20 years. He has chaired or co-chaired over 25 sessions at AIChE meetings, served on the AIChE Executive Board Programming Committee (2008–2011), and held officer positions in the Sustainable Engineering Forum. Dr. Hesketh has also been a Board Member of CACHE (2008–present) and contributed as Green Engineering Program Director for ASEE’s Chemical Engineering Division.
