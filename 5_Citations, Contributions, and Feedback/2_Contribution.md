    Dr. Kirti M. Yenkie is an Associate Professor of Chemical Engineering at Rowan University and leads the Sustainable Design and Systems Medicine lab. Her research and mentoring efforts are geared towards environmental sustainability by including practical examples and case studies from industry on water treatment, solvent recycling, petroleum pipeline cleaning, plastics recycling, and machine learning for early-stage life cycle analysis. She has developed educational modules, and videos for STEM outreach on Sustainability. She holds a Ph.D. in Bioengineering from the University of Illinois (Chicago, IL), M. Tech (Chem. Engg.) from IIT Bombay and B. Tech (Chem. Engg.) from Laxminarayan Institute of Technology, Nagpur. She has received the NSF CAREER award and multiple awards from the AIChE Divisions.

    Dr. Aditya Lele is an Assistant Professor of Mechanical Engineering at Rowan University, with a courtesy appointment in Chemical Engineering. His research uses ML-accelerated atomistic simulations to study dynamic, non-equilibrium, and reactive processes, with applications in plastic recycling, plasma catalysis, rapid joule-heated catalysis, and solid-state materials. He also teaches an interdisciplinary Practical Molecular Simulations course at Rowan.
